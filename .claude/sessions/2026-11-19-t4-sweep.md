## Shipped
- **Sim (opt-in, inert by default):**
  - A build purchase may carry `branch`, `maxTier` and `tag`, stored on the tower as `branchPref`, `tierCap` and `tag`. An upgrade purchase may carry `branch`.
  - The tier-4 key is `tier4${p.branch ?? t.branchPref ?? 'A'}`.
  - `upgradeCost` honours `tierCap` and `branchPref`, and greedy scoring reads `branchPref`.
- **`src/sim/t4Sweep.js`:**
  - `anchoredPlan(basePlan, { type, tier })` buys the anchor first at the top-coverage slot and holds gold until it can. It upgrades the anchor toward its tier, then hands the rest to the best strategy. It passes a towers copy with the anchor at its post-upgrade level and a fresh `slotsUsed`.
  - `runT4Sweep` and `sweepMaps` (maps with `maxTierAllowed` 4).
- **`src/sim/t4SweepReport.js`:** `formatT4Sweep` builds the rows, legend and #75 caveat. `npm run balance -- --t4` prints it after the normal report (about 35 s).

## Rulings
- Lives are taken at the baseline's win multiplier, not 1x. On maps 7–9 the baseline loses by wave 1–2 at 1x, so every row read 0%.
- Each row carries `@wN`: the wave the anchor reached its tier, taken from its own winning run. The final review found reach first came from the lives run, which hid real tier-4 wins (map 6 barracks 4A x1.27), and was one wave late. Both are fixed.

## Edge cases
- No branch, cap or tag anywhere → default output unchanged (golden fixture, default report diff).
- Anchor not affordable yet → returns `[]`, holding the gold.
- The anchor slot is excluded from the base plan's free slots in the same phase.
- A `tierCap: 3` anchor is never taken to tier 4 by greedy.
- The map's `maxTierAllowed` is still enforced.
- A tier bought in the final build phase still counts as reached.
- Tier-4 costs are equal across branches, so equal spend across 4A/4B is guaranteed.

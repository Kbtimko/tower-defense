# 2026-10-22 — Roadmap 2.2 side-rail HUD in game space

**Accomplished:** The user merged #101 (2.1). For 2.2 they chose **side rails** and **DOM in game space** over a literal Phaser-drawn HUD; I asked both questions. I mapped every canvas-width and HUD coupling, wrote `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-hud-rails.md` and ran it with SDD: 4 tasks, a fix round on tasks 1–3, then an opus final review and fix wave.

The geometry: a 1632×720 canvas with two 88-px columns per 176-px rail. The map sits in a camera viewport, so world coordinates are unchanged. The HUD is HTML in a scaled `#hud-layer`.

Bugs found and fixed along the way:
- the camera postFX vignette was measured over the wider canvas, so it was rebuilt in world space and pixel-matched to the old look;
- Phaser kept a stale canvas size after rotation, which the old bars had masked;
- taps over a rail acted on the map with stale world coordinates;
- the tower panel and inspectors could cover the rails;
- labels only fitted macOS font metrics;
- every DOM overlay was too small for touch.

The capture audit is now rail-aware, adds 844×390 and 932×430 devices, and enforces a touch-size rule.

**Decisions made:**
- **Rail width:** 176 per rail (two columns), not the 140 first pitched, so 15 controls plus stats and hero fit at ≥44 CSS px.
- **Coins:** clipped at the map edge on their way to the rail counter.
- **Story/Codex meta buttons:** now styled on desktop too.
- **Overlays:** the touch floor is always 44 px.
- **Pre-existing crash:** the tier-3 barracks panel crash (also on `main`) goes to backlog #44 and is not fixed on this branch.

**Decisions made (user):**
- The countdown starts at the wave's last spawn, and the wave sends at zero even into a busy board, so waves overlap (option B over "never send into a busy board").
- Countdown length is per map, longer where needed, for a bigger reward.
- Before wave 1: no auto-start. Pause, story dialog and codex freeze the countdown; the between-wave banner holds it.
- Skipping pays a small bonus for the seconds skipped.
- Ring badge. Off switch in Settings.
- If the sim says a map got harder, stretch its countdown.
- Costs the user accepted: long countdowns, skipped story beats, and one clear bonus when waves merge.

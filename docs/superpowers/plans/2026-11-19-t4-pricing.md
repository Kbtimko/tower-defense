**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `towerSpecAt(type, tier, branch)` → adds `shots` (default 1), `slow` (default 0) and `slowedMult` (default 1), folded tier over tier. `towerDpsAgainst(spec, weights)` multiplies by `shots × (slow > 0 && slowedMult > 1 ? slowedMult : 1)`.

  - "Volley is priced at 2 arrows": `towerSpecAt('archer', 4, 'A').shots === 2`. Its DPS equals 2 × the DPS of `{ ...spec, shots: 1 }`.
  - "Shatter spec inherits slow 0.2 from T3": `towerSpecAt('ice', 4, 'B')` has `slow === TOWER_DEFS.ice.tier3.slow` and `slowedMult === 2`. Its DPS equals 2 × the DPS of `{ ...spec, slowedMult: 1 }`.
  - "slowedMult without slow is not priced": `{ ...shatterSpec, slow: 0 }` has the same DPS as `{ ...shatterSpec, slow: 0, slowedMult: 1 }`.
  - "crowd effects stay unpriced": the Archmage (`mage`, 4, 'A') DPS equals the DPS of the same spec with no extra fields, i.e. `damage × fireRate` through computeDamage. Assert `towerSpecAt('mage', 4, 'A')` has no `chain` key.
